Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We have answers.

How do I track my package?

Once your rider picks up your package, you will receive a tracking link via WhatsApp. Click the link to view the rider's live location on a map in real-time.

What areas do you cover?

We currently cover the entire Owerri metropolis and its immediate surrounding areas. For deliveries outside Owerri, please contact us for a custom quote.

How do I pay for my delivery?

We accept Bank Transfers and Cash on Delivery. Payment details will be provided once you book a rider via WhatsApp.

Can I send fragile items?

Yes, our riders are trained to handle fragile items with the utmost care. Please ensure the item is properly packaged and let us know in advance if it is fragile.
